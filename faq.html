Frequently
asked questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope. Landing pages start from $500, business websites from $2,000, and custom web apps from $5,000. We provide a detailed proposal after understanding your needs. Contact us for a free quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

Landing pages take 1-2 weeks. Business websites take 3-6 weeks. Complex web applications take 2-4 months. We always provide a clear timeline before starting and keep you updated throughout.

Do you work with clients outside Lagos and London?

Absolutely. We work with clients globally. Our team operates across Lagos and London, and we serve businesses across Africa, Europe, the US, and beyond. Remote collaboration is in our DNA.

What technologies do you use?

We use modern, battle-tested technologies: React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, React Native, Flutter, and cloud platforms like AWS and Vercel. We choose the best stack for each project.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We offer maintenance packages, hosting support, and development retainers. We build long-term relationships and are always here when you need updates, fixes, or new features.

How do I get started?

Simply reach out via our contact page or WhatsApp. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your project goals, timeline, and budget. Then we'll send you a detailed proposal.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Of course. We frequently redesign and migrate existing websites. We'll audit your current site, identify what works and what doesn't, and create a modern, high-performing replacement.

Do you build e-commerce stores?

Yes. We build custom e-commerce solutions with secure payment integration (Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe), inventory management, and conversion-optimized design. See our e-commerce page for details.

Mobile App vs Website: Which Do You Actually Need?

Published July 2026  |  5 min read

"We need an app" is one of the most common things we hear. But do you actually need an app? Or would a website serve you better? The answer depends on your use case, budget, and goals.

Choose a Website When...

  • You need SEO visibility. Websites rank on Google. Apps don't (until you have 10K+ downloads).
  • Your content is public. Blog posts, portfolios, product catalogs -- websites are better for content-first businesses.
  • Budget is limited. A professional website costs $2K-$10K. An app costs $15K-$100K+.
  • You need broad reach. Anyone with a browser can access your site. No app store approval needed.
  • Speed to market matters. Websites launch in weeks. Apps take months.

Choose a Mobile App When...

  • You need device features. Camera, GPS, biometrics, push notifications, offline access.
  • User engagement is key. Apps have 3x higher engagement than mobile websites.
  • Recurring usage. If users open your product daily, an app provides a better experience.
  • Offline functionality. Apps work without internet. Websites don't.
  • You're building a platform. Marketplaces, social networks, and SaaS tools benefit from native apps.

The Smart Approach: Start with Web, Add App Later

Most successful businesses start with a website, validate their idea, then build an app once they have proven demand. This reduces risk and saves money. We call this the "web-first" strategy.

Cost Comparison

Website

Cost: $2K - $15K
Timeline: 2-6 weeks
Maintenance: Low
Reach: Universal

Mobile App

Cost: $15K - $100K+
Timeline: 2-6 months
Maintenance: High
Reach: App store only

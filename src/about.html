Who We Are: A Web Development Company

qiuQ is a web development company operating from two of the world's most dynamic tech hubs: Lagos and London. We combine the energy and innovation of Africa's tech scene with the global perspective of London's market.

As experienced developers and full-stack developers, we started with a simple idea: businesses need more than just a website. They need a technology partner who understands their goals, their customers, and the systems that connect them.

Today, our web development company helps startups, SMEs, and established brands design, build, and scale their digital presence. From custom web development and mobile apps to AI-powered automation, we handle the full digital lifecycle. See our portfolio for examples.

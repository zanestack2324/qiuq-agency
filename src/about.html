Who we are

qiuQ is a creative digital agency operating from two of the world's most dynamic tech hubs: Lagos and London. We combine the energy and innovation of Africa's tech scene with the global perspective of London's market.

We started with a simple idea: businesses need more than just a website. They need a digital partner who understands their goals, their customers, and the technology that connects them.

Today, we help startups, SMEs, and established brands design, build, and scale their digital presence. From custom web and mobile apps to AI-powered automation, we handle the full digital lifecycle.

Frequently Asked
Questions About working with us

Everything you need to know about pricing, timelines, and our process.

How much does web development cost?

Pricing depends on scope. Landing pages start from $500, business websites from $2,000, and custom web applications from $5,000. We provide a detailed proposal after understanding your needs. Request a free quote.

How long does development take?

Landing pages take 1-2 weeks. Business websites take 3-6 weeks. Complex web applications take 2-4 months. We always provide a clear timeline before starting and keep you updated throughout.

Do you work with clients outside Lagos and London?

Absolutely. We work with clients globally across Africa, Europe, the US, and beyond. Remote collaboration is in our DNA.

What technologies do you use?

We use the best tools for each project: React, Next.js, Vue.js, Node.js, Python, WordPress, Shopify, React Native, Flutter, and more. We choose the right stack for your specific needs.

Do you offer maintenance and support?

Yes. Our maintenance plans include hosting support, security updates, performance optimization, and ongoing development. We build long-term relationships and are always here when you need updates or new features.

How do I start a project with qiuQ?

Reach out via our contact page or WhatsApp. We schedule a free consultation to discuss your goals, timeline, and budget, then send a detailed proposal.

Do you offer SEO with web development?

Yes. Every website we build includes on-page SEO, schema markup, local SEO setup, and technical SEO from day one.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Of course. We audit your current site, identify what works and what doesn't, and create a modern, high-performing replacement. Contact us to get started.

Do you build e-commerce stores?

Yes. We build Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom e-commerce solutions with secure payment integration via Paystack, Flutterwave, and Stripe. See our e-commerce page for details.

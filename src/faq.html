Frequently Asked
Questions About website design

Everything you need to know about hiring our web design agency, website design pricing, and our web design process.

How much does professional website design cost?

Website design pricing depends on scope. Affordable website design for small businesses starts from $500 for landing pages, business website design from $2,000, and custom website development from $5,000. We provide a detailed proposal after understanding your needs. Request a free website quote from our web design agency.

How long does website development take?

Landing page design takes 1-2 weeks. Business website design takes 3-6 weeks. Complex web applications take 2-4 months. Our web design agency always provides a clear timeline before starting and keeps you updated throughout.

Do you work with clients outside Lagos and London?

Absolutely. Our web design agency works with clients globally. We serve businesses across Africa, Europe, the US, and beyond. Remote collaboration is in our DNA.

Should I hire a web designer or use a website builder?

Hiring a professional web designer from our web design agency gives you a custom, SEO-optimized website built for your specific business goals. Website builders are limited in design, SEO, and scalability. Professional website development delivers results that grow with your business.

Do you offer website maintenance services?

Yes. Our website maintenance services include hosting support, security updates, performance optimization, and continuous development retainers. Our web design agency builds long-term relationships and is always here when you need updates, fixes, or new features.

How do I hire a web designer from qiuQ?

Simply reach out via our contact page or WhatsApp. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your project goals, timeline, and budget. Our web design agency will then send you a detailed proposal.

Do you offer SEO services with website design?

Yes! Our website design services include on-page SEO services, schema markup, local SEO services setup, and technical SEO. Every website our web design agency builds is SEO-optimized from day one.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Of course. Our website redesign services include auditing your current site, identifying what works and what doesn't, and creating a modern, high-performing replacement. Contact our web design agency to get started.

Do you build e-commerce stores?

Yes. Our e-commerce website development includes Shopify website design, WooCommerce development, and custom builds with secure payment integration (Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe). See our e-commerce website development page for details.
